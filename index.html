5 · The turn

A year of coffee costs over $1,000. The coffee in it costs about $100.

Three people I interviewed, their yearly coffee spend (grey) and the beans inside it (pink).

“I would feel scammed and sad. But I wouldn't change my habits.”Emilia, Parsons
“You're paying for the whole experience.”Valentina, Fordham
“I'm paying way too much… a very big margin for the coffee shop.”Antonio, works in NYC

6 · The ask

Before your next $8 coffee, open your coffee app and check your yearly total.

Then decide if the experience is worth it. If it is, keep going, knowingly. If it isn't, try a cheaper coffee two days a week.
